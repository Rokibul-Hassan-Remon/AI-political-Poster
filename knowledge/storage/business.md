# Storage — Business

- A poster takes up to 3 photos (leader photos), ordered: photo 1 = main/center.
- Allowed: JPG, PNG, WEBP. Max 5 MB each.
- Only logged-in users can upload.
- Generated posters (PNG, PDF) are stored permanently so history can re-download them.
- Users are responsible for having rights to uploaded photos (terms note on the form).
